    Set up the homepage¶

    The homepage is a stack of blocks. Theme Styles › Homepage switches them on and off, and Page Builder holds their content. A block you switch off leaves no gap.

    Hero¶

    The hero reads the first slide of your carousel (Storefront › Home page carousel). Set the slide's image, heading, text, button label and link there. Only the first slide is shown; drag a slide to the first position to use it.

    • Image: at least 1920 × 1080, landscape, subject right of centre. The theme darkens the left side so the heading stays readable. On very wide screens the whole image is shown and its left edge fades into the background.
    • Accent word: wrap a word in asterisks, for example Fuel *your* game. It shows in your accent colour.

    Settings in Theme Styles › Homepage › Hero section:

    Setting Effect
    Show Hero Shows or hides the hero
    Small line above the hero heading For example Season 2026 Collection. Empty hides it
    Second hero button label / link An outlined button next to the slide's button
    Hero side panel What sits beside the hero: Stats, Quote, Gallery or Product (see below)
    Hero heading / description / button (used when the carousel is empty) A working hero before you add any slide

    The row of sport links under the buttons is a widget in the home_hero_links region. Edit or delete it in Page Builder.

    Hero side panel¶

    Choice Shows Content comes from
    Stats Three figures Page Builder region home_hero_stats
    Quote An athlete quote Page Builder region home_hero_quote
    Gallery A photo grid Page Builder region home_hero_gallery
    Product Your first featured product Nothing to set up

    An empty region hides the panel, and the hero copy then takes the full width.

    Flash sale strip¶

    A red strip with a countdown and a button.

    • Flash sale deadline takes a date and time such as 2026-12-31T23:59:59. Empty hides the strip; after the deadline the strip hides itself.
    • The label, heading and button are a widget in the home_flash_sale region.

    Free-shipping progress bar¶

    Free shipping threshold is the cart total at which shipping becomes free, as a plain number such as 150. The cart page and the cart drawer show a bar counting down to it. Empty hides the bar. Keep it in line with your real shipping rules in Settings › Shipping.

    Product sections¶

    Setting Effect
    Show Featured Products Products you marked Featured in the catalog
    Featured products: small line / heading / View all link The text above that section
    Show New Arrivals Your newest products
    New arrivals: small line / heading / View all link The text above that section

    The number of products in each section is set in Products › Number of products displayed.

    Other blocks¶

    Block Switch Content
    Trust bar (shipping, returns, secure checkout) Show Trust Bar Widget in home_trust_bar
    Sport links strip — Widget in home_below_trust
    Brand strip Show Brands Widget in home_brand_strip
    From the blog — Your three latest blog posts
    Shop the look — Photo grid widget in home_social_grid; the follow button links to your first social network
    Newsletter Show Newsletter BigCommerce newsletter sign-up

    Top bar¶

    The strip above the header, set in Theme Styles › Header and footer › Top bar: a message, an optional link text and the link URL.

    Avoid a fixed amount in the message ("over $150") unless it matches your real free-shipping rule and currency. The right side of the top bar holds the currency picker (shown when the store has two or more currencies) and the dark/light switch.

    Menu¶

    The header menu is your category tree. Top-level categories are the menu items. Hovering one opens a mega menu: each second-level category is a column title and its third-level categories are the links below. The header_mega_feature--global region adds a promo tile to the mega menu.
