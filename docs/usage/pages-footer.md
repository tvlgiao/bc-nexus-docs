# Pages, blog and footer

## Custom page layouts

Three layouts ship with the theme. Two are for **web pages** and one for a
**category**; they are not interchangeable.

| Layout | Applies to | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `nexus-shop-by-category` | Web page | A grid of every category with its image |
| `nexus-size-guide` | Web page | Size tables that read well on phones |
| `nexus-flash-sale` | Category | Discounted products with the countdown |

- **Web page:** **Storefront › Web pages**, edit the page, choose the layout
  under *Template layout file*.
- **Flash sale:** create a category such as *Sale*, add your discounted products,
  then set its *Template layout file* to `nexus-flash-sale` in
  **Products › Product categories**.

## Category pages

The banner at the top of a category shows the category image, name, description
and product count. Write a one-line description for each category in
**Products › Product categories**; without one the banner shows only the name.

## Blog

- **Blog page › Blog Posts Per Page** sets 6, 9 or 12 posts per page.
- Tags on your posts become filter chips above the list.
- Two Page Builder regions let you add content: `blog_below_content` under the
  post list and `blog_post_below_content` under each post.

## Contact page

Theme Styles › **Contact page** holds your support email, phone and opening
hours. They show beside the contact form and in the footer. The
`contact_below_form` region under the form suits an FAQ.

## Footer

- The link columns are the global regions `footer_column_1--global` to
  `footer_column_3--global`. Place a widget once and it shows on every page.
- `footer_brand--global` holds the short text under your logo, and
  `footer_bottom--global` the strip at the very bottom.
- Your email, phone and the address from **Settings › Store profile** appear with
  icons under the logo.
- Social icons come from **Storefront › Social media links**. Facebook, X,
  Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest, LinkedIn and TikTok get their own icon.
- The copyright line uses the current year and your store name.

## Dark and light mode

Shoppers switch mode with the button in the top bar, and the choice is kept on
their device. **Theme colors › Default color mode** sets what a first-time
visitor sees. Every colour has a dark and a light value; after changing a colour,
check both modes for readable text.
