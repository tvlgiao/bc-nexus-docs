# Install and apply

Nexus Sports needs no app and no extra plan beyond a standard BigCommerce
subscription.

## Add the theme to your store

1. In your control panel, go to **Storefront › Themes › Themes marketplace**.
2. Find **Nexus Sports** and click **Add to store**. The theme appears in your
   **Theme library**.
3. Click **Apply theme**, pick a variation, and confirm.

Your products, categories, web pages, navigation and settings carry over
untouched. The theme never changes catalog data. Your previous theme stays in
the Theme library, so you can switch back at any time.

## Plan-dependent features

Two features depend on your BigCommerce plan or settings. Without them the theme
still works and simply leaves them out:

| Feature | Needs |
|---|---|
| Filters on category pages (price, brand, size) | A plan that includes Product Filtering, and filters set up in **Products › Product filtering** |
| Gift certificates | Gift certificates enabled in **Settings › Gift certificates** |

## After you apply

- Upload your logo in **Storefront › Logo**. Without one, the store name shows as text.
- Add your social profiles in **Storefront › Social media links**. The footer
  shows an icon for each network you drag into *Live on your storefront*.
- Set your support email, phone and hours in Theme Styles › **Contact page**.
  They appear on the contact page and in the footer.
- Continue with [Choose a variation](choose-variation.md).
