# Troubleshooting

**The homepage hero is missing.**
Either *Show Hero* is off (Theme Styles › Homepage), or the carousel has no
slides and the fallback heading is empty. Add a slide in **Storefront › Home
page carousel** or fill in the fallback fields.

**The panel beside the hero is missing.**
Its Page Builder region (`home_hero_stats`, `home_hero_quote` or
`home_hero_gallery`) is empty, which hides the panel. Add a widget, or set
*Hero side panel* to *Product*.

**The flash sale strip disappeared.**
The deadline has passed, or *Flash sale deadline* is empty.

**Category pages show no filters.**
Product Filtering is not on your plan, or no filters are enabled in
**Products › Product filtering**. Category pages still show the category tree
and sorting.

**The footer has no social icons.**
Add your profiles in **Storefront › Social media links** and drag each network
into *Live on your storefront*. Filling in a link without dragging it does not
publish it.

**A setting will not save.**
Almost always the 64-character limit on theme settings. Shorten the text, or move
it into a Page Builder widget.

**Text is hard to read after I changed a colour.**
Colours are not checked when you edit them. Look at the page in both dark and
light mode after any colour change.

**My changes to theme files disappeared after an update.**
Updates replace theme files. Keep customisations in Page Builder, Theme Styles or
**Storefront › Script manager**, which updates do not touch.
