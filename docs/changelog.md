# Release notes

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Four variations: Track, Trail, Velo, Flow.
- Homepage with hero and side panel, flash sale countdown, trust bar, sport links,
  featured products, new arrivals, brand strip, blog and shop-the-look grid.
- 40 Page Builder regions, eight of them global.
- Product cards with sale, sold-out, low-stock, pre-order and custom labels,
  colour dots and a feature line from custom fields.
- Mega menu, quick view, cart drawer with free-shipping bar, grid and list view,
  product comparison, faceted search.
- Dark and light mode.
- Built on Cornerstone 6.21.0.

## Updating

Theme updates arrive in **Storefront › Themes**. Updating replaces the theme
files; your Theme Styles values, Page Builder content and catalog stay as they
are.
